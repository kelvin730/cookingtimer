Built for healthy routines

It helps the meals you planned actually happen.

Healthy eating often fails at the execution layer: too many steps, too many browser tabs, too many timers, and too much guessing. CookCue stays in the kitchen and turns cooking intent into calm, visible countdowns.

  • Meal prep salmon, eggs, broccoli, rice, chicken, and air-fryer meals.
  • Clarifies trigger moments like “after boiling” and “after preheating.”
  • Uses conservative guidance for meat, seafood, eggs, and high-risk foods.
  • Works as a simple manual timer when you already know the time.
